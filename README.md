# XAUUSD ICT Analysis

Website edukatif untuk menerapkan konsep **ICT / Smart Money Concepts** ke market real **XAUUSD (Gold)**.

## Fitur

- Ringkasan Market Snapshot
- Penerapan 6 strategi utama ICT:
  - Silver Bullet
  - Cameron’s Model
  - Turtle Soup
  - Optimal Trade Entry (OTE)
  - Inversion FVG & CISD
  - Power of Three (PO3) & CRT
- Skenario Bullish & Bearish praktis
- Panduan monitoring di TradingView
- Risk management notes
